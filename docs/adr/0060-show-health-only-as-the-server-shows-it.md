# ADR-0060: CX shows a creature's health only as the server's damage level.

Category: Interface.

Decided 2026-11-26.

## Brief

Health appears only as the damage level the server set, in its own caption and colour. CX reads no hit points and draws no bar, number or party frame of its own; the nameplate health bar and its settings are gone.

## Rationale

Some servers hide health on purpose and give other hints. A client that shows more hands its players an edge the server withheld, and a setting for it lets the player overrule the server.
